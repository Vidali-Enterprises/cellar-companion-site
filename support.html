Cellar Companion

Cellar Companion is an iPhone app for keeping track of your wine. Scan a label to add a bottle, see where each bottle sits in your racks and when it is ready to drink, find a wine to go with a dish, and share a cellar with the people you choose. Labels are read on your phone, and their photos stay there.

What to include in a report

  • What you were doing, step by step, and what you expected to happen.
  • What the app showed instead, with the exact words of any message.
  • The version and build shown under Settings → Version, and your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • A screenshot, if it shows the problem.

Please don’t include passwords, sign-in codes or other people’s details.
